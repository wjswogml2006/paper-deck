Is the research put in the context with existing literature?

3. Methodology (방법론)
What methodology is used?
Is it clearly explained?
Does theory make sense?
Is the theory appropriate to the research questions?
What data are used?
Are the data appropriate for addressing the research questions?
Does the sample reflect the overall population?
4. Results and Discussion (결과 및 논의)
What are the major findings?
Are the results clearly presented?
Is the interpretation of the results reasonable?
5. Conclusion and Implications (결론 및 시사점)
What are the major conclusions?
Can these conclusions be made based on the analysis?
What are the implications of the results?
Are they meaningful to managers or literature?
What did you learn from the paper?
6. Limitations and Future Research (한계 및 향후 연구)
What further research do you think could be done in this area?
What questions are still left by this research paper?
